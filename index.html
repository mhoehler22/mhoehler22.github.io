About

In 2023, I returned to school to pursue a master's degree in Data Science, looking to deepen the analytical work I'd been doing for nearly two decades. The program filled in the technical gaps, but it also connected back to the other parts of my background — the writing instructor, the musician, the communicator — and opened new directions in machine learning and generative AI. This portfolio brings those threads together through the projects I've completed along the way.

CFPB Complaint Clustering — Emerging Themes

Multi-stage NLP pipeline applying Ward and complete-linkage clustering to CFPB consumer banking complaints, then using LLM-based theme normalization and recency × semantic novelty scoring to surface emerging sub-type signals in single-company temporal data. The analysis identified a ~70% spike in complaints tied to a Wells Fargo fee policy change — a signal invisible in aggregate counts but detectable at the sub-theme level.

Media Metadata Graph

Builds a directed graph over synthetic personal media data — people, objects, memories, and media files (307 nodes, 632 edges) — to explore whether graph structures can surface narrative relationships that flat relational tables obscure. Applies Node2Vec embeddings, link prediction heuristics, t-SNE visualization, and K-Means clustering; a memory similarity retrieval function surfaces thematically related records without explicit labels.

Python NetworkX Node2Vec Pandas Jupyter Notebook

Starship Titanic — Kaggle Classification Challenge

Binary classification competition predicting which of 8,693 spaceship passengers were transported by a space-time anomaly. Applied domain-logic imputation, group-based feature engineering, and a five-model comparison before tuning CatBoost with Optuna Bayesian optimization. Achieved a public leaderboard score of 0.80523 (rank 517), with SHAP analysis confirming spending habits and cabin deck as the strongest predictors.

Python Pandas Scikit-learn CatBoost Optuna SHAP Jupyter Notebook
